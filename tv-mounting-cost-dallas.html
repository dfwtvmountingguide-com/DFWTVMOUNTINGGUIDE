TV Mounting Cost in Dallas

Dallas spans high-rise condos with metal studs, century-old East Dallas homes with plaster, and big North Dallas builds — and each affects a mounting quote differently.

Dallas wall types vary widely

Uptown high-rises often have metal studs; East Dallas has plaster-and-lath; North Dallas is largely standard drywall. Each needs different hardware and time.

Plaster walls take longer

Old plaster is brittle and harder to anchor cleanly — expect it to add a bit of labor.

Metal studs need special anchors

High-rise construction uses metal studs that require toggle or self-tapping anchors rated for the TV weight.

Condo and HOA rules

Some Dallas buildings require approval or pro installs. Check before booking.
