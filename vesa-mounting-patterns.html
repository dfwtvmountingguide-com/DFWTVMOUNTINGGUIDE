TV VESA Mounting Patterns Explained

VESA is the standardized mounting-hole pattern on the back of your TV. Matching it to your bracket is the difference between a clean install and a returned mount.

What VESA means

VESA is the distance (in mm) between the mounting holes — horizontal × vertical, like 200×200 or 400×400.

How to measure your TV

Measure the distance between the four holes on the back of your TV, center-to-center.

Common patterns

Smaller TVs use 100×100 or 200×200; larger TVs use 400×400, 600×400, or 800×400. Check the manual.

Match before you buy

Every mount lists its supported VESA range. Confirm your TV's pattern falls within it before purchasing.

Weight rating matters too

VESA fit isn't enough — the mount must also be rated for your TV's weight.
