Renter Guide

TV Mounting for Renters — Deposit-Safe Setup

Renters can mount a TV cleanly and still get the full deposit back. It's about fewer holes, clean patching, and reversible cable management.

Mount to studs with minimal holes

Stud anchoring is safer and uses fewer, cleaner holes than a wide toggle-anchor spread.

Patch before you leave

Small lag-bolt holes patch in minutes with spackle and touch-up paint — often invisible.

Removable cord covers

Surface raceways hide wires with adhesive and peel off without wall damage.

Get approval in writing

If the lease requires permission, get it in writing before drilling to avoid disputes at move-out.
