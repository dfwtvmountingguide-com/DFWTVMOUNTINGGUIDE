TV Size Guide

65-Inch TV Mounting Guide

The 65-inch is one of the most common TV sizes in DFW bedrooms and family rooms. It mounts cleanly with good planning and the right hardware.

Common mounting considerations

A 65-inch TV typically weighs 50–75 lbs. It usually fits well between wall studs, anchors securely with the right hardware, and looks great either centered or corner-mounted. Before outline work or ordering a mount, confirm your TV's VESA pattern and weight.

Viewing height

A standard seated viewing height target is 42 inches to the center of the screen. For a 65-inch that often means the bottom of the TV about 2–3 inches below the center height. Adjust for your sofa height and room.

Mount types for 65-inch

A fixed mount gives the cleanest, flush look. A tilting mount adds a small adjustable angle for reducing glare or accommodating a higher installation. A full-motion mount lets you pull the TV out and swivel it — useful for corner installations and smaller rooms. Choose based on whether you prefer the clean look of fixed, or the flexibility of full-motion.

Drywall and studs

65-inch TVs are light enough that many installers prefer to anchor into a single stud if possible or two studs for wider mount/base. The key is locating the studs accurately, drilling into the centers and using screws long enough to bite several inches into the wood. Metal studs are fine, but they require the specific anchors and equipment rated for metal framing.

Cable management for a 65-inch

A 65-inch wall-mounted TV looks best with the wires cleaned up: either a surface raceway or in-wall concealment. A proper in-wall cable kit is different from a rubbermaid speed bump; it uses a powered bridge or a low-voltage cable plate to bring power and HDMI behind the wall legally and cleanly.

Professional installation for 65-inch

65-inch is the size most homeowners feel is an easy DIY. People quickly regret their DIY when wires sag, drywall scraps are left in studs, or the TV is crooked.
