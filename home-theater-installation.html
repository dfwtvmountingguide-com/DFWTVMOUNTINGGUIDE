Home Theater Installation

Home Theater Installation in DFW

A true home theater is more than a TV on a wall — it's viewing height, soundbar placement, lighting and clean wiring together.

Screen placement and viewing distance

Larger screens need more distance. A 75- or 85-inch display usually calls for 8-12 feet of viewing distance in a home theater.

Soundbar and speaker integration

Center the soundbar under the TV, or plan in-wall speakers for a cleaner look. Cable routing for audio matters as much as for video.

Lighting and glare control

Fine lighting gives cinematic impact. Positioning the screen away from windows and using bias lighting improves the experience.

Cable management throughout

A theater room should look clean from every seat — including behind the TV and equipment rack.
