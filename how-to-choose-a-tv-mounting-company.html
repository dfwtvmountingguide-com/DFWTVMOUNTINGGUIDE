Hiring Guide

How to Choose a Local TV Mounting Company

The right installer makes the job safe, clean and hassle-free. Here's how to vet a local TV mounting company in the Dallas-Fort Worth area.

Check their service area

Make sure the company actually serves your city. Many installers focus on specific parts of DFW — Fort Worth and the Mid-Cities widely, others on North Dallas suburbs. A provider that covers the full metroplex (Fort Worth, Dallas, Arlington, Plano, McKinney and surrounding areas) gives you more scheduling flexibility.

Read the reviews

Look for consistent, recent, verified reviews on Google and similar platforms. A 5-star profile with lots of photos and a mention of installers by name is a strong signal. Watch how the company responds to negative reviews — that tells you their customer service.

Ask about insurance and liability

A professional should have general liability coverage so you're protected if something goes wrong (drop, damage, cracked drywall). While many local installers are small operations, the professional ones stand behind their work.

Confirm experience

Look for trained, experienced installers, preferably people who have mounted TVs of your size and on your wall type many times. Some jobs are simple; some above-fireplace or full-motion installs on stone need a hand who has done them.

Discuss your wall type

Every wall is different: drywall with wood studs, drywall with metal studs, brick, stone, plaster, concrete block. When you contact a company, mention your wall type. They can prep the right anchors, drill bits and approach before the appointment.

Ask about mounting equipment

A good installer typically brings quality, UL-rated hardware as part of the job. Ask whether they provide the mount, whether they bring tilting or full-motion options, and whether the price changes by hardware type. Some people buy their own mount; many prefer the installer to include it.

Confirm cable management options

If you want wires hidden, confirm before booking. Ask about external cable management (cord covers) vs. in-wall wire concealment. For in-wall, confirm they can route the cables safely and that their work is clean and legal. Wall-mounted TVs look far better with wire management.

Make sure they handle your TV size

Larger TVs (75-inch, 85-inch+) may need two installers, a full-motion mount, and a wall structure that can support the weight. Confirm the crew can handle your specific TV size and weight.

Clarify scheduling and timing

Find out: how far out are they booked? Same-week or next-week service? Do they offer windows of arrival? Confirm how long the install typically takes so you can plan around it. A professional operation gives you a clear window and shows up on time.

Verify the quote structure

Have them walk through cost before the appointment — 1 TV vs 2 TVs, fixed vs tilt vs full-motion mount, cable management included or extra, and any distance/travel fees. No surprises at the end.
