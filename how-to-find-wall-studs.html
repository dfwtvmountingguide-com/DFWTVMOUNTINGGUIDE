How to Find Wall Studs for TV Mounting

Finding studs accurately is the single most important step in a safe TV mount. Here's how to do it right — with a stud finder or without.

Use an electronic stud finder

A quality stud finder locates stud edges reliably. Mark both edges, then drill into the center between them.

Confirm with the knock test

Studs sound solid when tapped; hollow drywall sounds hollow. Use this as a cross-check, not the sole method.

Measure 16 inches

Studs are usually spaced 16 inches on center. Measuring from a known stud (or a corner) helps find the next.

Locate via outlets

Electrical outlets and switches are usually mounted to a stud on one side — checking that reveals a stud's edge.

When in doubt, call a pro

Hitting pipes or wires is the risk. In older DFW homes with unpredictable framing, a professional assessment is worth it.
