Apartment Guide

TV Mounting in Apartments & Rentals

Renters can enjoy a wall-mounted TV without forfeiting the deposit. The key is mounting to studs, minimizing damage, and using removable cable solutions.

Mount to studs, not anchors

Apartment walls have studs just like a house. Anchoring into studs is both safer and actually reduces drywall damage at move-out because you use fewer, cleaner holes.

Minimize holes

A single-stud mount uses far fewer holes than a wide plate. Smaller, cleaner holes are easier to patch with spackle before you leave.

Use surface cord covers

Skip in-wall power cuts. Paintable raceways hide wires cleanly and peel off at move-out with zero patching.

Check your lease first

Some leases require written approval or pro installation for wall mounts. Read the clause, get it in writing if needed.

Keep the mount

Mounts are removable and portable — pack it up and take it to your next place.
