Can You Mount a TV on Drywall?

Studs, anchors and the right way to mount a TV on a drywall wall.

Yes — with studs

Mount to wall studs behind the drywall, not to the drywall itself.

Find the studs

Use a stud finder, confirm edges, then drill pilot holes into stud centers.

Choose the right hardware

Lag bolts rated for the TV weight that come with quality mounts. Drywall anchors alone are not sufficient except for very small TVs.

Heavier TVs need a wider footprint

75" and 85" TVs should span at least two studs.

When to call a pro

Metal studs, plaster, brick? Pros have the right bits, anchors and experience.
