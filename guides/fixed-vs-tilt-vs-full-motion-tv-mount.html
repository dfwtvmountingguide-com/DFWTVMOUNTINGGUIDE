Fixed vs Tilt vs Full Motion TV Mount

How to pick between the three mount types for your room.

Fixed mounts

Slim, flush, fastest install, cheapest. No adjustability after mounting.

Tilt mounts

Angle down (or slightly up) to reduce glare; ideal for above-fireplace or higher installs.

Full motion

Pull-out, swivel, extend — best for corners or angled seating; cost and complexity higher.

Match mount to TV

Verify VESA pattern, weight rating and stud anchoring for your specific TV.

Room choice matters

Living room center: tilt. Corner: full motion. Bedroom: fixed or tilt.
