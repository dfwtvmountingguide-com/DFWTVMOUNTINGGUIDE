How Much Does It Cost to Mount a 75-Inch TV?

What affects the cost of 75-inch TV installations in DFW.

The size factor

75-inch TVs weigh 75–125 lbs — heavier than smaller TVs, which means more anchor points, a heavier mount, and more time.

Wall type drives cost

Drywall+studs is standard; brick, stone or metal-stud drywall adds labor time and hardware cost.

Mount type matters

Fixed mounts are cheapest; full-motion mounts with an 18-24 inch extension cost more to install correctly.

Wire concealment

Surface raceways add a small amount; in-wall power/HDMI runs affect both time and cost more.
