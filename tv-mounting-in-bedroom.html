Bedroom Guide

TV Mounting in the Bedroom

Bedroom TV mounting has different rules than the living room — mostly around height, because you're usually viewing from a lying-down position.

Bedroom height is lower

Since you view from lying down, mount the TV lower than a living-room set — roughly centered on your line of sight from the pillow.

Tilt for comfort

A tilting mount angles the screen down toward the bed, reducing neck strain.

Consider a full-motion mount

For rooms where you watch from bed and a chair, a full-motion mount lets you aim the screen at both spots.

Cable placement

Plan power and HDMI near the mount location — bedrooms often lack outlets at TV height.
