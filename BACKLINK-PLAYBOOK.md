9. Guest contributions to local blogs / neighborhood newsletters

     newsletter, realtor blog, or home-services roundup. Backlink from the byline.

============================================================

10. "DFW TV Mounting Cost" original data page

============================================================
WHAT NOT TO DO (self-inflicted penalties)
============================================================
- No buying links, no link farms, no link exchanges with spam sites.
- No exact-match anchor overuse. Vary anchor text naturally ("TV mounting cost in DFW",
  "the DFW TV Mounting Guide", the brand name, plain URLs).
- No fake reviews/ratings (also illegal + kills trust).
- Don't blast the same post to 50 groups in a day — organic pacing.
